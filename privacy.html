Privacy policy and terms

Cathra privacy & terms

Effective November 11, 2026. Cathra is operated by Teddy Aaron Ventures LLC. The short version: your image never leaves your device.

Your images

When you choose an image, your browser loads it into memory and the model analyzes it on your device. The image is never uploaded, sent to a server, saved, logged, or stored by Cathra. When you choose another image or close the page, it is discarded.

What we collect

Cathra has no accounts, no sign-up, and no forms. We do not collect your name, email, health information, or the results of any analysis. Cathra does not use advertising or cookies, and it does not track you across sites.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply, and we never sell or share it.

Visit counts

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits, such as how many people opened the tool. It uses no cookies, does not identify you, and does not follow you across sites. It never sees your image or your results.

Hosting and libraries

Like any website, the services that deliver Cathra’s files receive standard technical information when your browser requests them, such as your IP address, browser type, and the time of the request. These are:

  • Vercel, which hosts the website and the model files.
  • jsDelivr, a public CDN that delivers the TensorFlow.js and Teachable Machine libraries.

These requests are only for Cathra’s own code and model. Your image is never part of them. Each provider handles this information under its own privacy policy.
