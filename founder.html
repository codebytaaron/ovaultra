Founder

Teddy Aaron

Founder of Cathra · Greenwich, Connecticut

Photo: NBC Connecticut

Teddy Aaron is a student founder and product builder working where technology, healthcare, and artificial intelligence meet. He has built apps and platforms used by thousands of people, all while still in high school.

His health work focuses on private, on-device machine-learning tools that analyze medical images, including ovarian ultrasounds and blood cells. He built Cathra so students, researchers, and anyone curious could explore how AI reads ovarian ultrasounds for free, without ever uploading an image.
