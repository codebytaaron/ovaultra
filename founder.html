Founder

Teddy Aaron

Founder of Cathra · Greenwich, Connecticut

Photo: NBC Connecticut

Teddy Aaron is a student founder and product builder working where technology, healthcare, and artificial intelligence meet. He has built apps and platforms used by thousands of people, all while still in high school.

His health work focuses on private, on-device machine-learning tools that analyze medical images, including ovarian ultrasounds and blood cells. He built Cathra so students, researchers, and anyone curious could explore, for free, how machine learning compares ovarian ultrasound images to normal and abnormal patterns.

He also built Hemra, Cathra’s sister project, which compares white blood cell images to learned patterns, privately in the browser.
