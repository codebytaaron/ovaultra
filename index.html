Cathra AI Ovarian Ultrasound Pattern Research

Comparing ultrasound patterns, for research.

Cathra AI is a research and educational demonstration. It takes an ovarian ultrasound image that already exists — one captured with medical ultrasound equipment — and compares its visual pattern against two categories the model learned during training: a normal ovarian appearance and an abnormal ovarian appearance.

The result is a similarity score for each category, not a medical finding. An "abnormal-pattern" result can reflect entirely non-cancerous conditions such as a polycystic ovarian appearance, simple cysts, or complex cysts. A complex cyst or an abnormal result does not mean cancer.
