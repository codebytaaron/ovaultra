Cathra

Ovarian ultrasound pattern tool

Check how closely an ovarian ultrasound matches normal or abnormal patterns.

You upload an ovarian ultrasound image that was already taken with medical equipment. Cathra compares how it looks to two patterns it learned during training — a normal ovary and an abnormal ovary — and gives each a similarity score out of 100%. It does not diagnose anything, and the image never leaves your device.

How it works

  1. 1 Upload an image. Choose an existing ovarian ultrasound picture (JPG or PNG) from your device.
  2. 2 Cathra analyzes it. The image is resized and compared to the two learned patterns, entirely inside your browser.
  3. 3 You see two scores. A similarity score for the normal pattern and one for the abnormal pattern, plus whichever is higher.
  • Runs on your device — nothing is uploaded, saved, or stored
  • Accepts JPG and PNG ultrasound images
  • Shows similarity scores only — it is not a diagnosis
