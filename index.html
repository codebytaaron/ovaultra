Cathra Research demo

Ovarian ultrasound pattern research

Compare an ultrasound image against learned patterns.

Upload an ovarian ultrasound image that already exists. Cathra compares its appearance against two patterns it learned during training — normal and abnormal — and shows a similarity score for each. Everything runs in your browser; the image never leaves your device.

  • Runs locally — nothing is uploaded or stored
  • Accepts JPG and PNG images
  • Shows similarity scores, not a diagnosis
