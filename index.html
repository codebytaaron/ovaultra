Ovarian ultrasound pattern tool

Compare an ovarian ultrasound to learned patterns.

Upload an existing ultrasound image. Cathra scores how closely it matches a normal and an abnormal ovarian pattern, privately, in your browser.

How it works →
  • Trained on thousands of ovarian ultrasound images
  • Runs on your device
  • Nothing is uploaded or stored
  • Similarity scores, not a diagnosis

Why I built Cathra

Cancer has affected me personally. When I looked for tools like this, I found that work in this area is very limited, and there wasn’t a safe, free option anyone could explore. I saw that gap and built Cathra to fill it. I’m grateful for all the support, and hearing from people who’ve found it helpful means more to me than I can say. Thank you.

Teddy Aaron, founder
