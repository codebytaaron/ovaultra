To cite the tool itself: Aaron, T. (2026). Cathra (Version 2) [Computer software]. https://www.cathra.dev. If you use the training data, please also cite the PCOSGen dataset.

Teach with Cathra

A 45-minute lesson on what image AI can and can’t do in medicine.

  1. Introduce (10 min). A classifier compares an image to patterns it learned. Read How it works together.
  2. Explore (15 min). Students analyze a few ultrasound images and record the scores and any “Unable to assess” results.
  3. Discuss (15 min). Use the questions below.
  4. Reflect (5 min). Each student writes one thing a tool like this should never be used for.

Discussion questions

  • Why is a similarity score not the same as the chance someone has a condition?
  • Version 1 scored 74% overall but recognized only 19% of normal images. How can a single accuracy number be misleading?
  • Both versions were tested on images from one dataset. Why might results differ at another hospital?
  • Who should be responsible when a medical AI tool is wrong?
  • Why does it matter that Cathra runs on your device instead of a server?
