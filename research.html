Research & teaching

Cathra is free to use for learning and research. It is not for diagnosis or any medical decision.

Use Cathra in your class

A 45-minute lesson on what image AI can and can’t do in medicine.

  1. Introduce (10 min). What a classifier does: it compares an image to patterns it learned. Read the How it works page together.
  2. Explore (15 min). Students run the tool on a few images, then on something that isn’t an ultrasound, and note what happens.
  3. Discuss (15 min). Use the questions below.
  4. Reflect (5 min). Each student writes one thing a tool like this should never be used for.

Discussion questions

  • Why is a similarity score not the same as the chance someone has a condition?
  • The tool still scores a photo of a cat. What does that tell you about AI models?
  • What could go wrong if a model is trained on images from only one source?
  • Who should be responsible when a medical AI tool is wrong?
  • Why might it matter that Cathra runs on your device instead of a server?
